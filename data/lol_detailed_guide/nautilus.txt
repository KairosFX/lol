Champion: Nautilus

Primary Role
Flexible Specialist

Champion Identity
Versatile champion with multiple playstyle options

Abilities Overview
- Passive: Understand how the passive affects trading and scaling.
- Q Ability: Main damage or poke tool. Use carefully for wave control.
- W Ability: Utility spell used for mobility, crowd control, or survivability.
- E Ability: Secondary damage or setup tool for combos.
- Ultimate: Main playmaking ability. Track cooldowns carefully.

Strengths
- Strong mid-game pressure
- Good objective control
- Can snowball leads effectively
- Flexible itemization

Weaknesses
- Vulnerable when behind
- Cooldown dependent
- Can struggle against heavy crowd control
- Requires positioning discipline

Recommended Rune Setup
Primary Tree:
- Keystone suited for extended fights or burst depending on matchup
- Adaptive secondary runes for lane sustain or scaling

Secondary Tree:
- Defensive or utility runes depending on enemy composition

Core Item Build
Starting Items:
- Standard lane or jungle sustain start

Core Items:
- Mythic or core damage item
- Boots depending on matchup
- Situational survivability or penetration item

Situational Items
- Anti-heal against sustain teams
- Magic resist or armor against heavy damage threats
- Utility items for teamfighting

Laning Phase Tips
- Focus on wave management and safe trading
- Track enemy jungle pathing
- Use cooldown windows to pressure opponents
- Respect level spikes and all-ins

Mid Game Strategy
- Rotate for objectives and skirmishes
- Pressure side lanes when ahead
- Group when teamfight impact is needed
- Maintain vision around major objectives

Late Game Strategy
- Position carefully in teamfights
- Play around cooldowns and summoner spells
- Protect carries or dive priority targets depending on role
- Avoid unnecessary deaths before objectives

Combos
- Basic Combo: Use crowd control before committing burst damage
- Extended Combo: Chain abilities with auto attacks between cooldowns
- Teamfight Combo: Engage or follow up after frontline initiation

Power Spikes
- Level 6 unlock
- First completed core item
- Two-item mid-game spike
- Late-game scaling with full build

Teamfighting Advice
- Identify the highest-value target before committing
- Maintain spacing and positioning
- Save defensive cooldowns for critical moments
- Coordinate engages with teammates

Matchups and Counters
Good Against:
- Immobile champions
- Weak early-game scaling picks

Struggles Against:
- Heavy poke
- High mobility champions
- Strong disengage compositions

General Gameplay Advice
- Review replays to improve macro decisions
- Track objective timers constantly
- Adapt builds based on enemy threats
- Learn damage limits and engage ranges
